A practical difference

From finding a page to finding a reason to read.

Without content discovery

  • Reader searches manually or browses categories.
  • Relevant archive content is easy to miss.
  • Publisher sees page-level engagement, but limited explicit intent.

With Content Discovery

  • Reader provides intent and receives personalised stories.
  • Each recommendation explains why it matters.
  • Publisher sees selected interests, engagement and possible content gaps.

Works with your existing content

No migration. No CMS replacement.

Content Discovery connects to your existing publisher API or content source, normalises the content internally and links every recommendation back to the real article. Your CMS remains the source of truth.
