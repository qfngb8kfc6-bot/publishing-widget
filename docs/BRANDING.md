# Publisher branding

Publisher manifests can provide compact widget branding plus hosted experience branding:

- `branding.loading.desktopBackgroundImage` and `mobileBackgroundImage`
- `branding.report.desktopBackgroundImage` and `mobileBackgroundImage`
- fallback colour, overlay and text colour
- publisher logo, colours, CTA, terms/privacy URLs and optional powered-by treatment

Hosted pages use safe HTTP(S) assets, full-bleed cover behaviour, mobile alternatives and a fallback colour. No LD Systems image is required by the platform defaults.
